# Code of Conduct

UML Studio welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give specific and constructive feedback, and accept corrections. Make room for different languages, time zones, accessibility needs, and levels of experience. Critique code and ideas rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Repeated disruption, intimidation, or unwanted contact is also unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not publish sensitive incident details in a public issue. Request a private contact from the maintainer with a brief, non-sensitive issue titled **Request a private conduct contact**; no description of the incident or people involved is needed. A dedicated private conduct channel must be verified before actively recruiting a broader community (see the [publication checklist](docs/publication.md)). GitHub's own reporting tools remain available for violations of platform rules.

The maintainer may edit/remove content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or ban participants based on severity and recurrence. Reports should be handled confidentially to the extent possible, with disclosure limited to people needed to address the incident. An involved maintainer should seek an uninvolved reviewer where available. Decisions may be appealed through the same private channel with relevant new information. This small project does not promise an independent appeals board or a response-time guarantee.

See [governance](GOVERNANCE.md) for current responsibility.
