# Getting help

- **Setup or usage question:** open a [question issue](https://github.com/PKDGOOD/uml-desktop-client/issues/new?template=question.yml) with your OS, Node version, command, and sanitized error.
- **Bug:** use the [bug form](https://github.com/PKDGOOD/uml-desktop-client/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include a minimal synthetic example and expected/actual behavior.
- **Feature:** describe the workflow/problem in the [feature form](https://github.com/PKDGOOD/uml-desktop-client/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Security:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not disclose privately sensitive details in issues.

Search existing issues and [known limitations](docs/known-limitations.md) first. English and Korean are welcome. Support is best-effort; there is no guaranteed response time, paid support offering, or production SLA. Never attach company projects, access tokens, or personal information to reproduce a bug.
